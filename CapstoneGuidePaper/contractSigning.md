1. Applicant mag-aapply ng job
2. HR/Manager: Pending → Reviewed → Shortlisted → Interview Scheduled
3. Interview mangyayari (via app, Confirm/Can't attend)
4. HR/Manager: i-cclick ang status papuntang "HIRED"  ← dito nagsisimula lahat
5. Pag-Hired na, lalabas sa applicant's app ang 7 pre-employment requirements:
   - SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, NBI Clearance, Medical, TIN, at "Signed Employment Contract"
6. Applicant pipindutin yung "Signed Employment Contract" item → bubukas ang
   bagong Sign Contract screen → mag-sscroll muna siya para mabasa yung buong
   kontrata → saka lang mag-uunlock yung signature pad → pipirma gamit daliri
7. Pag-upload niya, makikita na ito ng branch manager/HR sa Applications page
   (ContractSignaturePanel — lumalabas lang kapag "Hired" na ang status)
8. Branch manager/HR mismo ay pipirma rin (countersignature) gamit yung sariling
   signature pad sa web admin
9. Pagkatapos ng dalawang pirma, kumpleto na ang kontrata — may contract_start_date
   at contract_end_date (batay sa contract_duration_months ng job)
