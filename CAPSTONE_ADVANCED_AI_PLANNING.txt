SECTION 1: PRE-EMPLOYMENT STEPS & ONBOARDING CHECKLIST WORKFLOW
================================================================================

1.1 OBJECTIVE:
    Provide an interactive pre-employment onboarding workflow for candidates who
    are "Shortlisted" or "Hired", guiding them through required pre-employment
    documents and government compliance steps before day 1 of work.

1.2 REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST:
    - SSS Number / E-1 Form
    - PhilHealth Member Data Record (MDR)
    - Pag-IBIG MID Number / Member Data Form
    - NBI Clearance / Police Clearance
    - Pre-Employment Medical Exam / Health Permit
    - BIR Form 1902 / TIN
    - Signed Employment Contract

1.3 DATA SCHEMA (Supabase PostgreSQL):
    Table Name: public.pre_employment_requirements
    Columns:
    - id (UUID, Primary Key)
    - user_id (TEXT, Foreign Key -> profiles.user_id)
    - application_id (UUID, Foreign Key -> applications.id)
    - requirement_name (TEXT, e.g., 'NBI Clearance')
    - status (TEXT: 'Pending', 'Uploaded', 'Verified', 'Rejected')
    - document_url (TEXT)
    - updated_at (TIMESTAMPTZ)

1.4 MOBILE APP INTEGRATION (Android Kotlin):
    - UI Fragment: PreEmploymentFragment.kt / ApplicationsFragment.kt
    - Progress Bar: "Requirements Completed: 4 / 7 (57%)"
    - Status Badges: Color-coded status for each requirement item.
    - File Picker: Upload PDF/Image for each requirement.

1.5 ADMIN WEB INTEGRATION (Next.js):
    - Page: /applications/[id]/requirements
    - HR Actions: Inspect uploaded government document PDFs/Images.
    - Status Controls: Click to 'Verify' or 'Reject' with feedback notes.

SECTION 2: HIRING VELOCITY ESTIMATOR & STRENGTHS/WEAKNESSES ML DIAGNOSTIC
================================================================================

2.1 OBJECTIVE:
    Provide predictive hiring intelligence showing estimated days to hire,
    accompanied by a structured ML diagnostic breakdown of candidate strengths
    and skill gaps.

2.2 HIRING VELOCITY ESTIMATOR ALGORITHM:
    - Base Hiring Timeline: 7 Business Days
    - Adjustments:
      * Match Score >= 85%: -2 Days (High urgency hire)
      * Complete Profile & Uploaded Resume: -1 Day
      * Complete Pre-Employment Documents: -1 Day
      * Lacks core skills (< 50% match): +3 Days
      * Low experience level mismatch: +2 Days
    - Output Format: "Estimated Hiring Velocity: 2 - 4 Business Days"

2.3 STRENGTHS AND WEAKNESSES ML DIAGNOSTIC ENGINE:
    Class Name: CandidateDiagnosticEngine.kt

    A. STRENGTHS EXTRACTION (Mga Kasanayan at Bentahe):
       - Core Skill Matches: Matches between candidate skills and job requirements.
       - Experience Fit: Candidate years of experience vs required level.
       - Geographic Fit: Lives within Metro Manila branch boundary.
       - Profile Completeness: Pre-employment compliance ready.
       - Example Outputs:
         [+] "Strong Cash Handling & POS expertise (3+ years)"
         [+] "High TF-IDF similarity in Store Management & Inventory Auditing"
         [+] "Location match: Pasig City (Branch Proximity)"

    B. WEAKNESSES / SKILL GAPS (Mga Kulang na Kasanayan):
       - Missing Core Skills: Key requirements listed in job description missing from candidate profile.
       - Experience Gap: Insufficient years of experience for senior roles.
       - Missing Certifications: e.g., Forklift license, First Aid certification.
       - Example Outputs:
         [-] "Lacks listed requirement: Forklift Operation Certification"
         [-] "No prior experience listed in Visual Merchandising"
         [-] "Incomplete pre-employment NBI Clearance"

SECTION 3: EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) RANKING EXPLANATION ENGINE
================================================================================

3.1 OBJECTIVE:
    Provide transparent, human-readable explanations detailing WHY a candidate
    was ranked at a specific position and match percentage.

3.2 XAI EXPLANATION ENGINE SPECIFICATION:
    Class Name: ExplainableRankingEngine.kt

    Inputs:
    - UserProfile (skills, experience, headline, location, resumeText)
    - Job (title, category, level, aboutRole, requirements)
    - MatchResult (matchScore, vectorSimilarity)

    Generated Explanation Template:
    "Candidate [Name] is ranked at #[Rank] with a [Score]% Match for [Job Title].
     This ranking is driven by a [Similarity]% text similarity in [Category] roles,
     matching [MatchedCount] out of [TotalReqs] primary job requirements ([SkillsList]).
     Candidate experience of [Years] years aligns with the [Level] requirement."

3.3 UI DISPLAY:
    - Mobile App: Expandable "Why this match?" card in RecommendedJobsFragment.kt and JobDetailFragment.kt.
    - Admin Web: Candidate Ranking Breakdown card in /applications page.
